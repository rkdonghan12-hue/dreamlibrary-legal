How to play

Take an uncovered piece from the pile and drag it to a tray. Pieces keep their orientation. Fill all 24 squares to clear a tray. Empty the pile to finish the stage.

Help with a move

You can reposition a piece in its tray or move it to another tray. An extra tray lasts only for the current attempt. Restarting or entering the next stage closes it.

Get help

Parents and guardians can email the stage number, app version and a description of the issue. Please leave out the child’s name, photo and other personal details.
