Privacy policy

Donghan Cao • Updated November 27, 2026

Your game on this device

PilePack is a general-audience puzzle game. You can play offline without a developer account. Local profile labels, board moves, unlocked stages, each stage’s best result, play statistics, language, music and sound settings stay in the app on this device. The developer operates no gameplay or analytics server and has not integrated a separate gameplay analytics service. Removing the app and its data clears these records. A device backup may restore them.

Optional Game Center leaderboard

If you sign in to Apple Game Center and link a local profile, the game sends Apple the sum of your best star results across stages. Apple associates the score with your Game Center identity and stores it for the global, all-time ranking. Other players can see your Game Center display name, total and rank. The game keeps the game-specific player identifier and last synchronized total locally to credit the correct profile. It does not send Apple your local profile label, moves or stage-by-stage history. You can play without joining. You can manage your Game Center account and privacy in Apple Settings.

Optional rewarded ads

You can choose a video for an extra tray, an undo or, on eligible stages, a guide. Unity Ads starts only after the arithmetic confirmation and only on eligible iOS installations. Every live request selects contextual ads, declines personalized advertising and signals opt-out. Skipping a video earns no reward. We do not pass Game Center identifiers, scores or local game records to Unity. Unity and its service and advertising partners may process the IP address and approximate country, device or installation identifiers, language, device and network characteristics, ad impressions, interactions, usage and SDK performance. These support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and SDK operation. Unity may receive a report if you report an ad. Unity describes international processing, including in the United States, and retains data according to its purposes and legal obligations.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, the developer uses your email address and the details you send to answer your request. You may use the same address to request access or deletion. Removing this app clears local records separately from records held by Apple or Unity. Their privacy notices below explain their controls.
