Privacy policy

Donghan Cao • Updated November 24, 2026

On the device

The game works without a player account. It saves player labels, stage progress, local rankings, settings and play statistics on the device. These records are not published on an online leaderboard. Deleting the app and its data removes its local records; a system backup can restore them.

Optional video rewards

An adult can choose to start an ad after a parental gate. Until then, the game does not initialize Unity Ads. We request contextual advertising and opt out of personalized advertising. Unity describes processing an IP address and an installation-specific identifier when serving contextual ads to children. The game does not send player labels or board history to Unity. Skipping the video gives no reward.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact us, we use the email address and details you provide to answer your request. A parent or guardian may ask about this information or request its deletion by emailing the developer. Local game records can be removed on the device.
