Privacy Policy

Daily Oracle (占卜每天 · بصيرة يومية) · donghan.cao · rkdonghan12@gmail.com

Effective date: November 13, 2026. This policy applies to the Daily Oracle app and the online services it uses.

Version 0.37.2 amendment: November 25, 2026.

Operator and contact

The app is operated by donghan.cao (individual developer). For support or privacy questions, contact rkdonghan12@gmail.com.

Local records and chosen online actions

The app stores diary records, habit lists and settings on your device. Requesting an online reading, generating a check-in message or choosing to share sends only the information needed for that action. Device backups, keyboards and sharing destinations you choose are controlled by Apple and the providers you select.

Information used online

Almanac requests contain a date, language and traditional calendar fields. Check-in messages use the habit title, today's count and streak. Dream and fortune reading requests are not processed until the online reading service is activated; when it is, the sending screen will identify the provider and the fields being sent and ask for your consent before sending. Signed transactions, request identifiers and device verification data may be processed to deliver purchases and enforce the weekly device allowance.

Voice and permissions

Built-in voice input uses on-device speech recognition for the selected language and does not save an audio recording. Typing remains available if recognition is unsupported or permission is denied. Motion sensors are used only for the shake-to-draw gesture. Saving share artwork uses add-only photo permission; the app does not read your photo library. System keyboard dictation follows the system or keyboard provider settings.

Providers and international processing

The almanac and check-in service is hosted in Singapore and calls the MiniMax API (api.minimax.cn) to generate daily content and encouragement messages. See MiniMax's official site (www.minimax.cn) for the provider's own terms and policies. Apple operates payment and subscription services. Network and cloud providers process connection information as required to deliver the service.

Retention and deletion

Server-side almanac and check-in content is cached for about six hours to serve repeated requests. We keep server data only as long as needed to provide the service and to enforce the weekly device allowance. You may request access, correction or deletion at rkdonghan12@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days. Do not include unnecessary dream details, passwords, banking details or identity documents in a deletion request.

Tracking and purposes

Version 0.37.1 does not contain an advertising SDK. Version 0.37.2 includes preparation for Unity Ads; rewarded ads are currently disabled on our server. The daily gift campaign does not require an ad. No ad SDK initialization is requested by our app until a supported-device user voluntarily chooses an available ad and confirms a separate ad-data notice. Before any future activation, we will update the applicable privacy disclosures. Unity may process network, IP-derived approximate location, device and app identifiers, ad views, interactions and diagnostic data when its service is used. We do not send dream text, diary records, fortune text or Apple transaction proofs to Unity. We do not sell personal data. See Unity’s privacy policy.

Your requests

Contact rkdonghan12@gmail.com about access, correction or deletion. We verify requests without asking for unrelated sensitive information.

Children and changes

The app is for general audiences; see the App Store product page for the assigned age rating. If this policy changes, we post the updated version at the same address. Where a change requires new consent before sensitive data is sent, we ask for it again.

Daily gift and voluntary poster sharing

During the one-calendar-month campaign displayed in the app, each verified device can use one free dream OR fortune reading per Riyadh calendar day (UTC+3), shared between both types, without rollover. No ad, purchase, rating, invitation or social post is required. DeviceCheck and protected device credentials help enforce the quota. Dream text and feelings are sent to MiniMax only after separate consent; fortune requests use the stick number and language. Completed reading results are encrypted on our server and recoverable for up to seven days; minimal transaction and device anti-abuse records may remain to enforce access and handle disputes. Choosing a poster-sharing shortcut opens the iOS share sheet; only your chosen artwork and its public App Store link are passed to the destination you select. Platform availability depends on installed share extensions, and opening a menu does not publish anything. We do not inspect your social account or require proof of a public post.
